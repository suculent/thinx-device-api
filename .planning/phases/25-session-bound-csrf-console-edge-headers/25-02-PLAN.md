Prepare the two console-facing pieces that later production plans depend on, without deploying anything.

1. **Tracer (Task 1).** The classic AngularJS dashboard (`app/js/thinx-api.js`, plain `$.ajax`) sends no `X-XSRF-TOKEN` today, so every dashboard mutation would 403 once its route is guarded, because `CSRF_ENFORCE=true` is already live.
