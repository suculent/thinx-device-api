- The Phase 23 re-proof build (device "Fridge", build `17d30770-bb80-11f1-b777-2975311bbbc4`, a private repository) succeeded on 2026-09-28. - The worker logs `[info] worker runArgv …` for an accepted argv job; `failJob` refusals carry `Invalid job authentication` or `Missing job secret`.
