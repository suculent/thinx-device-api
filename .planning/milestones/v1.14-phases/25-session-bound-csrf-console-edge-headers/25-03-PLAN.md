<objective>
Make the observe stage useful and the exemption correct, then give operators the tools the rollout plans use.

1. **Tracer (Task 1).** In observe mode a binding failure reaches the handler, logs one reason-coded line and increments a durable Redis counter (logs vanish when a task is rescheduled; research Pitfall 7). Persisted sessions with a pre-deploy or foreign token get a bound token minted lazily, and duplicate cookies are cleaned (Pitfall 5).
2. **Exemption (Task 2).** `router.js` marks verified Bearer and API-key requests with a request-local `req.thx_auth`, `verifyCsrfToken` exempts only those (D-09), the Bearer bridge keeps its session id, and the stray Google callback session write goes (Pitfall 11).
3. **CI spec, runbook and probes (Task 3).** `ZZ-CSRFEnforceSpec.js` runs the enforce-mode flow against the real app in CI. The runbook documents CSRF_MODE operation and rollback (D-08). `scripts/csrf-live-probe.sh` and `scripts/csrf-obs-counters.js` are the evidence tools of plans 25-04, 25-06, 25-08 and 25-10.

Purpose: SEC-CSRF-02 (observe telemetry, re-mint), SEC-CSRF-03 (no regeneration on the Bearer bridge, token login rotation in CI), SEC-CSRF-05 (Bearer / API-key exemption). Implements D-06 (as amended by D-17), D-08, D-09 and the D-21 note on `router.google.js`.
Output: code and spec changes, two operator scripts, the runbook section. Nothing is pushed in this plan.
</objective>
