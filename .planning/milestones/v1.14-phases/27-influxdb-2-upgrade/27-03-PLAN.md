<objective>
Make the dashboard and Visits statistics really render from InfluxDB 2, stop persisting attacker-supplied values in stats, and give production a read-only, aggregate-only probe.

1. **Tracer (Task 1).** Seeded points in InfluxDB 2 go through `InfluxConnector.week/today` → `Statistics.week_V2/today_V2` → `Util.responder` and become the exact `{success:true, response:{KPI:[n]}}` JSON that the Vue dashboard reads. Research F-1 shows these routes answer "no results" today whatever is stored.
2. **D-12 (Task 2).**
   - APIKEY_INVALID stops carrying the rejected key.
   - LOGIN_INVALID labels are allow-listed.
   - Both are proven by spec.
3. **Probe (Task 3).**
   - Add read-only connector helpers and `scripts/influx-stats-probe.js`.
   - Production steps (27-05..27-07) prove counts, retention and writes with it, inside the API image, without printing secrets.

Purpose: success criterion 2 (non-zero dashboard), D-10, D-12, and the evidence tooling for success criteria 1, 2 and 4.
Output: statistics.js fix, apikey and router.auth hardening, the probe, and four specs.
</objective>
