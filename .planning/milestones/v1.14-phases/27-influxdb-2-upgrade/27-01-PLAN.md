<objective>
Take and prove the safety net before the irreversible storage upgrade, and rehearse that upgrade on production hardware without touching the live service.

1. **Tracer (Task 1).**
   - Read-only pre-flight.
   - Portable backup of every 1.8 database from the running `thinx_influxdb` container.
   - A second copy on the other node, checked against one sha256 manifest.
   - A restore into a throwaway 1.8 container on micro, with counts equal to production at the backup time.
2. **Rehearsal and runbook (Task 2).**
   - Run `influxd upgrade` from the DHI image against a copy of the restored data, exactly as the cutover will.
   - Start a scratch v2 server from the result, compare counts, and check that the rename to `stats` with 90d keeps the DBRP mapping.
   - Tear everything down except the backup.
   - Write the full Phase 27 runbook.

Purpose:
- Success criterion 1, backup half ("taken and verified restorable").
- The folded todo.
- D-02: the backup is kept until D-07.
- D-05 and D-08: the upgrade path is proven from the production image before the cutover decision.

Output: the backup on two nodes, annex rows "pre-flight", "backup", "restore" and "rehearsal", and the runbook.
